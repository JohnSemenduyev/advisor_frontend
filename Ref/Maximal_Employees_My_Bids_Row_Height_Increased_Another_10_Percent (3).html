Evaluation Criteria Guide

Performance CategoryQuestions to Consider When Evaluating
Attendance
Does the officer arrive on time and report for all scheduled shifts?
Are absences, late arrivals, and early departures infrequent and properly communicated?
Does the officer follow call-out and scheduling procedures?
Appearance
Does the officer consistently wear the complete, clean, and properly fitted required uniform?
Are grooming, identification, footwear, and equipment compliant with company and site standards?
Does the officer present a professional image?
Professionalism
Does the officer treat clients, visitors, coworkers, and the public respectfully?
Does the officer follow post orders, maintain appropriate conduct, exercise sound judgment, and handle difficult situations calmly?
Responsiveness
Does the officer promptly respond to dispatch, supervisors, client requests, alarms, and incidents?
Are reports and updates timely, accurate, and complete?
Does the officer escalate urgent issues without unnecessary delay?
Other
Are there additional job-related strengths or concerns not covered above, such as initiative, teamwork, adaptability, site-specific duties, or training completion?
Are observations supported by specific examples?
